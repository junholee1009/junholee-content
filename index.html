I’m Junho Lee, an exchange student at the University of Missouri–Kansas City studying Information & Communication Engineering. I created these short-form projects to explore how everyday campus experiences, culture, and community can become engaging stories for students.

Why I Made These

I have not yet held a formal social media or marketing position, so I wanted to demonstrate my interest through action. Experiencing UMKC as an international student made me curious about the stories in everyday campus life. I turned that curiosity into two short-form projects, exploring how people, culture, and events can become approachable content—and reflecting on what makes a story relevant to students.

Behind the Content

  2. Audience
  3. Story

I start by identifying what I want the audience to notice or feel. From there, I use visual choices, pacing, editing, and copy to make the idea easy to understand and engaging to watch.

03 / THE PERSPECTIVE

What I Bring

01

International Perspective

As an exchange student, I experience UMKC as both a community member and someone seeing it for the first time. That perspective helps me notice stories, details, and experiences that may otherwise feel ordinary.

02

Technical Mindset

My engineering background has trained me to approach unfamiliar problems systematically, learn new tools quickly, and think carefully about how information is structured and communicated. I bring that same curiosity and care to creating content.

03

Learning by Creating

I learn best by making things. These projects allowed me to move beyond interest and practice content planning, filming, editing, collaboration, and audience-focused storytelling—then reflect on what I could improve in the next project.
